Add it to Gmail

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon (top right), then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "Rossetta".
  3. Click inside the signature box and paste (Cmd + V on Mac, Ctrl + V on Windows).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
